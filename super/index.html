What is this? Super Dangerous Dave is a modified build of the reconstruction of Dangerous Dave (John Romero, Softdisk, 1990): the game's source code was rebuilt from the DOS program, compiled for the browser, and given ten new levels and time that runs backwards. Hold Shift and everything that happened happens in reverse - Dave, monsters, shots, pick-ups, the score, even a death and the restart - back to the start of the level and the levels before it; let go and play goes on from there. The ten levels of 1990 are still here as the Classic set. High scores and best times are kept in this browser. The plain game.
